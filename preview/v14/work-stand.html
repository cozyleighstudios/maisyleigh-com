Zen MagSafe Charger Stand

A weighted phone stand, shaped after a Zen garden.

Year
2024
My part
Idea, form, colors
Made with
Tristan, who engineered it. Printed and polished in our studio.
Size
15.5 × 9 × 10 cm, about 283 g
“Cozy tech that actually fits my vibe. I had to design it myself.”

Every charger I could find was black plastic. Nothing matched the rest of my desk.

Where the garden idea came from, and the first shapes on the iPad.

What tipped over, what looked wrong, and what we changed.

Inside
A steel core, so it doesn't budge when you tap your phone.
Finish
Polished by hand.
Colors
A new palette each season.

It has been on my desk every day since.
