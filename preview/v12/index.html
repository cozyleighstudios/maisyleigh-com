About

I wanted tech to feel warm.

When I started filming my desk in 2020, most tech videos were black, white and minimal. I made mine bright and cozy.

In 2024 I left my job to design and make the pieces myself. Today I run Cozyleigh Studios with my family: a designer, an artist and an engineer. I still film my desk, and now I film the studio too.
