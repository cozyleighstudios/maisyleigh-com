Eight desk tips

From years of sitting at a desk six to eight hours a day.

  1. Start with your mind, not your desk.A few minutes of journaling with a warm drink before work makes the rest of the day feel lighter.
  2. Set the mood with light.Open the curtains in the day. When the sun's gone, a soft, warm lamp keeps it cozy.
  3. Bring in something living.Plants are a gentle nudge to slow down. Pick planters you love looking at.
  4. Choose one warm palette.Mine comes from nature: maple wood, creamy fabrics, earthy greens. Let everything follow it.
  5. Fill it with you.Art from people you love and small things with meaning. That's what makes it yours.
  6. Tidy for five minutes at night.A clear space leads to a clear mind, and tomorrow's desk feels kinder.
  7. Move every hour.Get up, stretch your wrists, and look at something 20 feet away to rest your eyes.
  8. Let notifications wait.Your tech should fit your life, not the other way around. Journal before you scroll.
