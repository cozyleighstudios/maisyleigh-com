Zen Light

A paper-lantern desk light, designed with Gantri.

Year
2023
My part
Concept and design
Made with
Gantri, who make and ship it.
A lantern for the desk. Soft light, in a shape you can stack.

I wanted the warm light I film in, in a piece small enough to sit beside a monitor.

It started on paper, at my desk.

I folded small models to check the proportions before anything was made.

Then the design went on screen, and back and forth with the team at Gantri.

Made by
Gantri, in California. They make and ship each one.
Where
On a desk, a shelf or a side table.

On the shelf above a desk.
